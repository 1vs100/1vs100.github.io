# One against a hundred, live again

1 vs 100 was a live game show on Xbox Live — thousands of players answering
the same questions at the same time. The official service ended in 2010; this
community project runs the show again. [More about the project](about.md).

</div>

## Pick your path

- **You have a modded Xbox 360** → [Xbox 360 setup](xbox-360.md): copy a few
  files to the console and start the game.
- **You want to play on a PC** (Windows or Linux) → [PC setup](pc.md): point
  the game at your files, export your Avatar, and play.
- Not sure which fits you? [Platforms & requirements](platforms.md) compares
  them in ten seconds.
