# Platforms & requirements

Two ways into the same shows. Pick the one you have — the setup guide for
each lists exactly what you need.

## Xbox 360

A modded console (RGH or JTAG) with DashLaunch and your own copy of the
game. The classic way to play, on original hardware.

→ [Xbox 360 setup](xbox-360.md)

## PC (Windows / Linux)

The rebuilt PC version plays online together with Xbox 360 players.
Needs your own game files from your console — the same packages as
Xbox, one copy serves both.

→ [PC setup](pc.md)
